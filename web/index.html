A note is a beat long: c4, f#4, eb3. A length in beats goes after a colon: c4:2, g4:.5. A dot is a rest, - holds the note before, | is a bar line for your eyes only. A double bar line || begins a new part: the first is A, the next B, and the form below says in which order they are played.

The written parts in the style

One or two to a bar, bars parted by |: C, Am, G7, Dm7, Fmaj7, Bdim. A - leaves a bar to the arranger.

Starting…

Click adds a note, dragging moves it, its right end lengthens it, a double click removes it. A double click on a bar number begins a new part of the tune there (B, C…), or joins it to the part before. Keys A to K play C to C (W E T Y U the black keys, Z X the octave), . is a rest, Backspace takes the last note back.

The notes will be drawn here: grey for what was in the source, colour for what the chip plays.
